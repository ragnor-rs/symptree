## Requirement

Make symptree the **scheduler of record** for the pilot: the internal `MedicalDataSource` (FR-YDANPW) owns the appointment calendar, and the symptomizer's `BOOK_APPOINTMENT` conclusion books the patient on a **first-available** slot computed from the clinic's own doctor schedules — no external HIS on the critical path. This supersedes the operator-handoff model of archived FR-4GCYJK and pulls the `first_available` strategy of FR-2VQ7CA forward, but computed *internally* rather than delegated to a HIS.

When a conclusion has `clinic_action == BOOK_APPOINTMENT`, the backend finds the earliest matching slot across active doctors carrying the conclusion's specialist code, and the bot **auto-assigns that earliest slot and asks the patient to confirm** (`"Доктор Иванова — 15 июня 10:00 — подтвердить? да/нет"`, project-owner decision 2026-06-11). On confirm, a `bookings` row is written (double-booking prevented at the DB), and the FR-4GCYJK reminder + post-visit-feedback infrastructure fires unchanged. On decline, the bot offers the next-earliest slot.

The `bookings` schema is rewritten **source-agnostic**: the HL7-flavored `hl7_appointment_id NOT NULL` (technical-spec.md § 2.1) becomes a nullable `external_appointment_id` paired with `source_id` + `external_doctor_id` (FR-YDANPW leak cleanup AC-YDANPW.3).

References: archived FR-4GCYJK (reminder/feedback/`bot-internal/outgoing` infra reused verbatim; operator-handoff superseded); FR-YDANPW (the `MedicalDataSource` + doctor schema this builds on); FR-2VQ7CA (`first_available` strategy, slot-strategy seam); technical-spec.md § 2.1 (`bookings` schema rewrite), § 4.5 (reminder scheduler); brainstorm 2026-06-11 (internal scheduler, auto-assign + confirm).

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-W03362.1: `InternalScheduler` implements both `SchedulingProvider` and `BookingProvider` (FR-YDANPW) over the internal tables. `availability(doctorRef, window)` is a **pure function** of `doctor_weekly_hours − doctor_time_off − confirmed bookings`, subdivided into `slot_minutes` grid slots — unit-testable with no DB. **Time model:** `doctor_weekly_hours.start_time`/`end_time` are **local wall-clock** anchored by the clinic's IANA timezone (`clinics.config.preferences.timezone`, default `'UTC'`); the scheduler resolves `(date, weekly-hours row, clinic zone) → UTC Instant` and evaluates `day_of_week` (0=Sun..6=Sat) in clinic-local time, so emitted `Slot`s are UTC and DST-correct (IANA zone, not a fixed offset). Trailing remainder shorter than `slot_minutes` is dropped. `slot_minutes` is a single per-doctor duration (no per-service durations in M2). See technical-spec.md § 3.4 *Scheduling time model*.
- AC-W03362.2: `BookingService.findFirstAvailable(clinicId, specialistCode, from, to)` queries active doctors whose `specialist_codes ∋ specialistCode`, calls `availability` per doctor, and returns the earliest remaining slot across all of them (tie → doctor with most free capacity in the window). Pluggable behind a `SlotStrategy` seam with `FirstAvailable` as the only MVP strategy (`round_robin` / `least_busy` deferred to FR-2VQ7CA).
- AC-W03362.3: Migration `database/07-migrate-bookings.sql` creates `bookings` **source-agnostic**: `id`, `clinic_id` FK, `session_id` FK, `source_id`, `external_doctor_id TEXT`, `doctor_identity_id` FK (nullable — display/aggregation), `specialist_code`, `slot_at TIMESTAMPTZ`, `slot_end TIMESTAMPTZ`, `external_appointment_id TEXT` (nullable — null for internal source), `status` (`confirmed | cancelled | completed`), `feedback_score SMALLINT`, `feedback_text TEXT`, `created_at`, `updated_at`. No column named `hl7_*`.
- AC-W03362.4: Double-booking is prevented at the DB by a partial exclusion constraint scoped to internally-owned calendars: `EXCLUDE USING gist (external_doctor_id WITH =, tstzrange(slot_at, slot_end) WITH &&) WHERE (source_id = '<internal>' AND status = 'confirmed')` (requires `btree_gist`). A concurrent double-book attempt on the same internal doctor+overlap raises a constraint violation surfaced as a typed `SlotTakenException`.
- AC-W03362.5: `SessionService.applyStep` on a `BOOK_APPOINTMENT` conclusion calls `findFirstAvailable`; if a slot exists it returns a `StepResult.ConfirmSlot` rendering catalog key `bot.booking.confirm_slot` (params: doctor display name, slot time) with yes/no buttons; if none exists in the lookahead window it returns `bot.booking.no_slots`. Session flips to a `pending_booking` state holding the proposed slot.
- AC-W03362.6: On patient **confirm**: backend (a) inserts the `bookings` row via `InternalScheduler.book` (exclusion constraint enforces no-double-book; on `SlotTakenException` re-runs `findFirstAvailable` and re-proposes), (b) inserts a pre-visit reminder (`due_at = slot_at − 24h`, `kind = 'pre_visit_24h'`, catalog key `bot.reminder.pre_visit_24h` + doctor/slot/prep params), (c) inserts a post-visit feedback reminder (`due_at = slot_end + 2h`, `kind = 'post_visit_feedback'`), (d) sends `bot.booking.confirmed`, (e) flips session to `concluded`. Same-day edge case: `slot_at − 24h` in the past → reminder `due_at = now() + 1 min` with catalog key `bot.reminder.same_day`.
- AC-W03362.7: On patient **decline**: backend re-runs `findFirstAvailable` excluding **every slot declined so far this conversation** (accumulated in `pending_bookings.declined_slots TIMESTAMPTZ[]`, migration 11) and proposes the next-earliest (`bot.booking.confirm_slot` again); excluding the whole declined set — not just the latest — stops the re-proposal cycling back to an already-rejected time. After a configurable N declines (default 3) the bot falls back to `bot.booking.contact_clinic` and flips the session to `concluded`.
- AC-W03362.8: Migration `database/08-migrate-reminders.sql` creates `reminders` (`id`, `clinic_id` FK, `session_id` FK, `due_at`, `kind`, `payload JSONB`, `status`, `sent_at`, `created_at`; index on `(due_at) WHERE status='pending'`). `ReminderScheduler` coroutine (launched from `CoreApp.main` on `Dispatchers.IO`): polls every 60s `SELECT … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED LIMIT 50` on `reminders WHERE status='pending' AND due_at < now()+interval '60s'`, builds the outbound payload (`platform_user_id` + session `language` + `catalog_key` + `params` from `reminders.payload`) and POSTs it to messaging's `/bot-internal/outgoing` — which renders via its **own** catalog per AC-W03362.9 (**Variant A**, project-owner decision 2026-06-11: rendering is a view-layer concern; this supersedes the earlier "core renders the catalog string" wording here and in technical-spec.md § 4.5, and is generalized to the ingress path by FR-VWRNDR) — then marks `sent`/`failed`. Survives restarts; past-due rows picked on startup. (Reused from archived FR-4GCYJK AC.7.)
- AC-W03362.9: `POST /bot-internal/outgoing` on the messaging module: shared-secret authed via `BOT_INTERNAL_SHARED_SECRET` (distinct from the ingress secret); body `{platform_user_id, language, catalog_key, params}`; renders + calls `MessagingPort.sendText`. 401 wrong secret, 422 missing key, 200 on send. (Reused from archived FR-4GCYJK AC.8.)
- AC-W03362.10: Post-visit feedback: when the `post_visit_feedback` reminder lands, the bot sends `bot.feedback.prompt` (1–5). `FeedbackHandler` (not the LLM router) interprets the next message within 24h: integer 1–5 → `bookings.feedback_score` + `feedback_text`; out-of-range/non-numeric → one reprompt, then welcome menu. (Reused from archived FR-4GCYJK AC.9.)
- AC-W03362.11: Two-instance concurrency test: two `ReminderScheduler` coroutines against shared Postgres, 100 due rows, each sent exactly once (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`). (Reused from archived FR-4GCYJK AC.10.)
- AC-W03362.13: **Minimum booking lead time.** `findFirstAvailable` (and the AC-W03362.7 decline re-propose) start their availability window at `now() + min_lead`, never proposing a slot earlier than that. `min_lead` is `clinics.config.preferences.min_lead_hours` hours, **per-clinic, default 24** (resolved at the wiring layer like the clinic IANA zone of AC-W03362.1). `min_lead_hours: 0` re-enables same-day/near-now proposals (the `same_day` reminder path of AC-W03362.6 then applies); larger values enforce a later-booking policy ("tomorrow or later" at the default). Rationale: a patient cannot attend an appointment minutes away; auto-assign must respect a realistic booking horizon. The `BookingService` lead is a `Duration` defaulting to `ZERO` (so the pure-unit default proposes the literal earliest slot); production wires the clinic-config default of 24h.
- AC-W03362.12: End-to-end `AutoBookingFlowIT`: seed clinic + algorithm + 2 doctors with weekly hours → drive symptomizer to `BOOK_APPOINTMENT` → assert earliest slot proposed (`bot.booking.confirm_slot`) → patient "да" → assert `bookings` row (`source_id=internal`, correct `external_doctor_id`, `external_appointment_id IS NULL`) + two `reminders` rows + `bot.booking.confirmed` → fast-forward via `Clock` double → pre-visit reminder fires → fast-forward to `slot_end + 2h` → post-visit prompt → reply "5" → `feedback_score = 5`. Concurrency variant: two patients race the same last slot → exactly one `bookings` row, the loser is re-proposed the next slot.

## Technical Design

Builds on FR-YDANPW (`SchedulingProvider`/`BookingProvider`, `source/internal/`), archived FR-4GCYJK's reminder/feedback design, technical-spec.md § 4.5.

- **Module layout:** `source/internal/InternalScheduler.kt`, `booking/{BookingService,SlotStrategy,FeedbackHandler}.kt`, `scheduler/ReminderScheduler.kt`, `db/{BookingRepo,ReminderRepo}.kt`. The messaging module keeps `/bot-internal/outgoing` (no new `MessagingPort` method — payload is `catalog_key + params`).
- **Slot computation is pure:** `availability` takes `(weeklyHours, timeOff, existingBookings, slotMinutes, window)` and returns `List<Slot>`. The DB read is in the repo layer; the algorithm is a pure function so `SlotStrategy` and edge cases (split shifts, DST boundaries, partial-day time-off) are unit-tested without Testcontainers.
- **Exclusion constraint, not app-level locking:** double-booking prevention is the Postgres `EXCLUDE` constraint (the write-side equivalent of the reminder poll's `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`). Scoped `WHERE source_id = internal` because external calendars are the vendor's authority — symptree's mirror rows for external bookings carry no exclusion guarantee.
- **No `hl7_*` anywhere:** the bookings rewrite is part of FR-YDANPW's leak cleanup; `NoProviderNameLeakTest` (AC-YDANPW.9) sweeps `07-migrate-bookings.sql`.
- **Reused-verbatim infra:** ReminderScheduler, `/bot-internal/outgoing`, FeedbackHandler, the same-day edge case, and the two-instance concurrency guarantee are lifted from archived FR-4GCYJK with no behavioral change — only the *trigger* changes (internal auto-book replaces operator PATCH confirm).

## Testing

Per testing-spec.md.

- `InternalSchedulerTest` (pure, no DB) — split shifts, time-off subtraction, slot-grid subdivision, existing-booking exclusion, empty-window → no slots.
- `SlotStrategyTest` — `FirstAvailable` picks earliest; tie-break by free capacity.
- `BookingRepoIT` (Testcontainers) — insert, exclusion-constraint rejection on overlap, cross-clinic isolation, `external_appointment_id` nullable for internal.
- `BookingServiceTest` with fakes — confirm/decline/no-slot paths; `SlotTakenException` re-propose loop; **minimum-lead-time window** (AC-W03362.13: a slot inside `now()+min_lead` is excluded, the earliest slot at/after the horizon is proposed, and the decline re-propose honors the same horizon).
- `FeedbackHandlerTest` — 1–5 parse, out-of-range reprompt, post-window fallback.
- `ReminderSchedulerIT` — single-instance happy + two-coroutine exactly-once + same-day catalog-key swap.
- `AutoBookingFlowIT` + the race variant per AC-W03362.12, against Testcontainers + `FakeLlmRouterClient`.

## Notes

- Patient `PatientRef` stays Telegram-id-only (PII-light) for internal-source booking. The fuller `PatientRef` (phone, full name, MRN) is needed only when an external `BookingProvider` demands HL7v2 ADT^A04 — that lands with the second source (FR-2VQ7CA / M3).
- For external sources later, `bookings` is a **mirror-on-write** row carrying `external_appointment_id` so reminders/feedback/analytics work uniformly across sources. Reconciliation of changes made *directly* in a vendor system (stale mirror) is a known later-FR gap — non-issue for the internal-only pilot.
- The operator-driven "book in the panel" alternative (closer to FR-4GCYJK) is not the MVP path — booking is bot-driven auto-assign. Panel-side manual booking/override can be a fast-follow.
